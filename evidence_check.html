Why do self-ratings and task scores differ?

A discrepancy between self-ratings and lab scores is completely normal and expected during undergraduate career exploration. Coursework tests broad conceptual memorization, whereas practical scenarios test subtle syntax edge cases and domain heuristics. PathWise never silently overwrites your self-rating; we provide this tool to help you calibrate your self-awareness respectfully.
